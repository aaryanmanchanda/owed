<objective>
Confirm Bedrock is genuinely usable from ap-south-1 and prove the single riskiest technical path in this whole project — a Lambda reading image bytes out of S3 and calling Bedrock directly — one full day before Phase 2 depends on it.

Per D-05 this smoke test was added to Phase 1 specifically to retire HANDOFF §13 risk 2 (IAM plus S3-bytes-into-Bedrock is the steepest learning curve here) early. Per D-06 the Lambda mediates: it calls `s3.get_object` itself and then `bedrock.converse`, and Step Functions' direct Bedrock image integration is never used. Per D-07 the pass bar is a schema-valid response and nothing more — accuracy belongs to Phase 2's real 30-photo evaluation and must not be measured or claimed here. Per D-08 the model is whichever candidate grants inference access first; do not spend Day-2 time comparing the two. Per D-09 none of this can run until the new account from D-03 authenticates, which plan 01-01 established.

The throwaway Lambda lives at `src/_smoketest/`, deliberately NOT at `src/extract/` — that path is Phase 3's production extraction module and must not be confused with this (D-06).

Purpose: satisfies ROADMAP Phase 1 success criterion 2 and closes RESEARCH open question 1.
Output: a recorded inference-profile ID and its destination-region set, plus a deployed, invoked, schema-validated smoke-test Lambda.
</objective>
